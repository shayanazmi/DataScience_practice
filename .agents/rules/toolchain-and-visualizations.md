# Local Toolchain & Proactive Visualizations Rule

1. **System Engines Available**:
   - **Manim Community (`manim`) & FFmpeg**: Installed locally for generating 1080p/4K animations of neural network architectures, forward/backprop dynamics, optimization loss surfaces, and mathematical concepts.
   - **TeX Live (`latex`, `pdflatex`, `dvisvgm`)**: Installed locally for native LaTeX formula typesetting and publication-grade PDF report exports from Jupyter notebooks (`nbconvert`) or Markdown documents.

2. **Proactive Assistance Guidelines**:
   - **Concept Visualizations**: When exploring or analyzing complex ML architectures, mathematical derivations, or data science workflows, proactively suggest when an animated 1080p Manim scene could provide high visual clarity.
   - **Lab & Project Reports**: Proactively offer to compile notebook writeups or project reports into clean LaTeX PDFs when preparing deliverables or documentation.
   - **Workspace Cleanliness**: Ensure visualization scripts and rendered media are organized neatly without cluttering primary course/project directories.
